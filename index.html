FursaLink Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

FursaLink ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile app that connects students and graduates with internship and job opportunities posted by companies. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using FursaLink you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you register we collect your full name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see or read your password.

Profile information. Students and graduates may add details such as education, skills, experience, contact details, and documents such as a CV. Companies may add company details and opportunity postings. You choose what to add.

Activity in the app. This includes applications you submit, opportunities you post, ratings, notifications, and messages you send in chats (student and company chats, and company and admin support chats).

Subscription records. For company accounts, we keep subscription status, validity dates and payment history records entered by the administrator.

Technical information. Our service providers (see section 4) may automatically process technical data such as device and app identifiers and IP address in order to deliver and secure the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your email address with a 6-digit confirmation code.
  • To match students with opportunities, let companies review applicants, and enable chats between users.
  • To send in-app notifications and service-related emails, such as confirmation codes.
  • To manage company subscriptions and provide support.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. What other users can see

When a student applies to an opportunity, the company that posted it can see the student's application, profile information and documents the student submits. Messages in a chat are visible to the participants of that chat and to administrators where needed for support and safety. Please share only information you are comfortable sharing.

4. Service providers

FursaLink is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage and Cloud Functions). Your data is stored and processed by Google on our behalf under Google's terms and privacy practices. Confirmation-code emails are sent through Gmail. We share data with these providers only as needed to run the app, and not for their own marketing.

5. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and associated personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records for legal, security or accounting reasons.

6. Your choices and account deletion

You can update your profile information inside the app. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and data, email us at fursalink.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my FursaLink account" (or "Data request"). We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard protections provided by Firebase, including encrypted connections, access rules on stored data, and hashed storage of email confirmation codes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

FursaLink is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: fursalink.app@gmail.com
