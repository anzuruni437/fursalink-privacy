Delete Your FursaLink Account

FursaLink is an internship-matching app that connects students and graduates with companies. This page explains how to request deletion of your account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to fursalink.app@gmail.com.
  2. Use the same email address you registered with in the app, so we can verify the account is yours.
  3. Use the subject line: Delete my FursaLink account.

We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login (name, email address, password credentials)
  • Your profile information and any files you uploaded (such as a CV or profile photo)
  • Your messages, applications and ratings linked to your account

What may be kept

  • Records we are required to keep for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes (for example, subscription payment records for company accounts). These are kept only for as long as required and are not used for any other purpose.

Questions

If you have any questions about your data or this process, contact us at fursalink.app@gmail.com.
